PROCEDURE:

Patient underwent bilateral total knee arthroplasty with patellar resurfacing under general anesthesia. The procedure included preparation of both femoral and tibial surfaces, cement fixation of prosthetic components, and closure in layers.

OPERATIVE NOTE:

The patient was brought to the operating room and positioned supine. General anesthesia was administered. Both lower extremities were prepped and draped in sterile fashion. A midline anterior incision was made over each knee. The joint capsule was opened, and the arthritic joint surfaces were exposed. Bone cuts were made using standard templates. Trial components were placed to ensure proper fit and alignment. The definitive prosthetic components were cemented in place. The patella was resurfaced bilaterally. Wound irrigation was performed, and closure was accomplished in layers with absorbable sutures.
